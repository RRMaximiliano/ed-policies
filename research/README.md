# Research staging files

Raw output of the June 2026 research pass that expanded the catalog from 65 to 101
policies (four regional sweeps: Southern Cone, Andean, Central America,
Mexico/Brazil/Caribbean). These entries have already been merged into
`src/data/policies.json`, which is the single source of truth; the files here are kept
only as provenance for that pass. Do not edit them expecting the site to change.

Every entry was researched against live web sources at the time of writing; references
that could not be verified were omitted rather than guessed. Evidence ratings were
assigned conservatively per the definitions in CONTRIBUTING.md.
